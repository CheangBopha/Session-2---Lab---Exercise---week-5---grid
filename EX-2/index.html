Angkor Wat

Angkor Wat is the most famous temple in Cambodia. It is a UNESCO World Heritage Site and attracts visitors from around the world.

Royal Palace

The Royal Palace is located in Phnom Penh and is known for its beautiful Khmer architecture.

Koh Rong Island

Koh Rong is famous for its white sand beaches, clear blue water, and relaxing atmosphere.

Tonle Sap Lake

Tonle Sap is the largest freshwater lake in Southeast Asia and supports many floating villages.

Bokor Mountain

Bokor Mountain offers cool weather, scenic views, and historical buildings in Kampot Province.
